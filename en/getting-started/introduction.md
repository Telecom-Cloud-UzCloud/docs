# Introduction

Welcome to the **UzCloud** documentation! Manage and optimize your cloud infrastructure with the UzCloud console.

> [!TIP]
> The UzCloud console is available at **[console.uzcloud.uz](https://console.uzcloud.uz)**.

## 📖 About UzCloud

**UzCloud** is a cloud management platform that gives you a single interface for provisioning, monitoring, and managing your cloud resources: virtual machines, volumes, networks, Kubernetes clusters, object storage, DNS, backups and more.

With UzCloud, organizations can simplify the operation of their cloud infrastructure, increase operational efficiency, and gain real-time insights into the resources they use. The UzCloud infrastructure is built on **Apache CloudStack**.

## Features of UzCloud

- ☁️ **Cloud resource management** — create and manage virtual machines, storage, and networks from one place.
- 📊 **Real-time monitoring** — track and analyze resource usage with real-time metrics.
- ⚡ **Automated scaling** — automatically scale cloud resources based on demand.
- 💰 **Cost control** — keep track of your cloud expenses with billing and cost analysis tools.
- 🔒 **Advanced security** — protect your account with two-factor authentication, firewalls, and activity logs.

**UzCloud** centralizes operations and empowers cloud administrators to manage resources more efficiently, monitor critical infrastructure in real time, and take proactive steps toward improving the scalability and cost-efficiency of their cloud environments.
