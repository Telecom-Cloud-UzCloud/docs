# Profile Setup

## Setup User Profile in UzCloud

The **UzCloud** profile lets you manage your personal and company information, interface preferences, security settings, API access, users and roles, quotas, and account activity. To open it, click your name in the top-right corner of the console.

![Profile Page](../../images/profile-page.png)

### Update Your Personal Information

Keep your personal details up to date so that your profile reflects accurate information.

- Open **Personal Details** in the profile menu.
- Click the pencil icon to edit the details.
- Update **Name**, **Address Line 1**, **Country**, **State** and **City**.
- Click **Submit** to save changes.

![Personal Details](../../images/profile-personal-details.png)

- To change your email address or mobile number, click **Change** next to the field in the **Profile Details** block. You will need to complete verification for the new value.

### Manage Company and Billing Information

Accurate billing information is essential for invoices and payments.

- Open **Company Details** in the profile menu.
- Click the pencil icon to edit the details.
- Fill in **Organization Name** and the **Billing Details**: billing address, website, country, state, city, INN (taxpayer identification number), billing name, billing email and an additional billing email.
- Save the changes.

![Company and Billing Details](../../images/profile-billing-details.png)

### Customize Account Preferences

- Open **Account Preferences** in the profile menu.
- In **Theme mode**, choose **Light Mode** or **Dark Mode**. The change is applied instantly.
- In **App Language**, choose the console language: **English**, **Russian** or **Uzbek**.

> [!NOTE]
> Screenshots and interface element names in this documentation are shown for the English version of the console.

![Account Preferences](../../images/profile-account-preference.png)

### Change Date and Time Settings

- Open **Date & Time** in the profile menu.
- Choose your **Preferred Time Zone** and **Preferred Date Time Format**.
- Click **Submit** to apply the changes.

> [!NOTE]
> The selected time zone and format are used for display only. All processing is done in the Asia/Samarkand time zone.

![Date and Time Settings](../../images/profile-date-and-time-settings.png)

### Secure Your Account with Two-Factor Authentication

Enhance security by enabling **Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)**: each time you sign in, you will need to enter your password and a one-time code sent to your email.

- Open **Authentication** in the profile menu.
- In the **Email Authentication** block, click **SET UP**.
- Confirm your email address and enter the one-time code (OTP) sent to it.
- To require 2FA for all users of your account, turn on the **Enable** switch in the **2FA Enforcement Status** block.

![Two-Factor Authentication Setup](../../images/profile-auth-two-factor-auth.png)

### Change Your Password

- Open **Authentication** in the profile menu and click **Change Password**.
- Enter your **Current Password**, then enter and confirm the **New Password**.
- Click **Change Password** to save changes.

![Change Password](../../images/profile-change-password.png)

### API Access

The **API** section lets you work with UzCloud through the API using a personal access token.

- Open **API** in the profile menu.
- Click **Enable API** to create a **Personal Access Token**. The token expiry date is shown under the token; use the copy icon to copy it.
- To revoke API access, click **Disable API**.
- Links to the API documentation are shown at the top of the page.

> [!WARNING]
> Keep your token secret: anyone who has it can manage your resources.

![API Access](../../images/profile-api.png)

### Add a New User

The **Users** section lets you add users to your account and assign roles to them.

- Open **Users** in the profile menu. The list shows each user's name, email, role, creation date, last login and status.
- Click **+ Add New User**, enter the user details, select a role and save.
- Use the **Actions** menu to edit a user or resend the invitation.

![Users](../../images/user.png)

### Assign User Roles and Permissions

The **Roles** section shows the available roles and their permissions.

- Open **Roles** in the profile menu.
- Select a role to view its description and permissions. Predefined roles: **Owner**, **Service Administrator** and **Service Viewer**.
- Click the **+** button to create a new role with custom permissions.

![Roles](../../images/roles.png)

### View and Increase Quotas

The **Account Limit** section (the **Quota** page) shows resource limits and usage for each zone.

- Open **Account Limit** in the profile menu.
- On the **Quota Limits** tab, select a zone to see the limit, usage and available amount of each resource.
- Click **Request To Increase** next to a resource to request a higher limit. The request status is shown in the **Request Status** column.
- The **Quota Usages Report** tab shows a usage report.

![Quota](../../images/req.png)

### Track Activity Logs

The **Activity Logs** section records actions performed in your account: IP address, user, project, action type, description, time and status.

- Open **Activity Logs** in the profile menu.
- Use the search bar to filter logs by keyword.

![Activity Logs](../../images/activity.png)

### Review Login History

The **Login History** section shows all sign-ins to your account, including unsuccessful attempts.

- Open **Login History** in the profile menu.

![Login History](../../images/loginhist.png)

### Conclusion

By following this guide, you can manage your UzCloud profile, strengthen account security, and configure the console to suit your needs.
