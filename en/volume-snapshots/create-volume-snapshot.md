# Create Volume Snapshot

## Volume Snapshot

A **volume snapshot** captures the state of a volume at a specific point in time. You can restore the volume from it after accidental deletion, data corruption, or a failed update.

> [!NOTE]
> Snapshots are billed at UZS 3.00 per GB per hour. A snapshot can only be restored to a disk of equal or larger size.

---

### Creating a Volume Snapshot

- From the left-hand menu, click **Snapshots** and open the **Volumes Snapshot** tab. The page shows all snapshots, including manually created ones and snapshots generated by schedules.
- Click **+ Create New**. The **Create Snapshot** page opens.

![Volume Snapshots](../../images/create-volume-snap.png)

- Select the **project** and the **location** where the volume is located.

![Choose Project](../../images/create-snapshot-project.png)

- In **Choose Instance or Volumes**, open the **Volumes** tab and select the volume.

![Choose Volume](../../images/create-volume-storage-snap.png)

- Enter a unique **Snapshot Name**.
- Check the **Price Summary** and click **Review And Create Snapshot**.

![Create Snapshot](../../images/create-snapshot-name.png)

### Conclusion

Volume snapshots are a simple way to protect your data. To create snapshots automatically, use **Schedules**. For further assistance, refer to the UzCloud documentation or reach out to support.
