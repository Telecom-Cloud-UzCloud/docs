# Egress Rules

## Egress Rules

An egress rule controls outbound traffic from the network to a specified destination, based on the protocol and address ranges.

- On the network page, open the **Egress rules** tab. It lists the egress rules of the network.
- Click **Add Egress Rule**.

![Egress rules](../../../images/pub-net-3.png)

### Add an Egress Rule

- Enter the **Source CIDR** and **Destination CIDR**.
- Select the protocol: **All**, **TCP**, **UDP** or **ICMP**.
- Click **Add Egress Rule**.

![Add an egress rule](../../../images/pub-net-6.png)

### Conclusion

**Egress rules** let you control which outbound traffic can leave your network, improving security.
