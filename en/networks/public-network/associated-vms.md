# Associated VMs

## Associated VMs

The **Associated VMs** tab on the network page lists the VMs connected to the network: status, location, project, operating system, IP address and configuration. Use the search to find the right VM.

![Associated VMs](../../../images/pub-net-5.png)

### Conclusion

The **Associated VMs** tab gives a clear view of all VMs linked to the network, helping you monitor and manage connected resources.
