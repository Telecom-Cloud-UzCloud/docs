# Support

## Support

The **Support** page of the UzCloud console lets you open tickets, track their status and contact the support team.

- In the console header, open the **Support** page.

![Support](../../images/support-1.png)

### Submitting and Managing Tickets

- In the **Raise a Ticket** form, select the **Ticket Type**, the **Priority** and, optionally, the **Service** your request is about.
- Enter the **Subject** and **Message**. If needed, attach files (drag them into the form or click **Browse files**).
- Click **Submit**.
- The **My Tickets** section shows the number of open and closed tickets and your latest tickets. Click **View All** to see all tickets and their status.

### Need Immediate Assistance

- If you need immediate help, contact customer service by email at **[info@uzcloud.uz](mailto:info@uzcloud.uz)** or by phone at **[+998 77 718 76 70](tel:+998777187670)** (every day, 8 AM to 9 PM).
- Keep your CRN (Customer Reference Number) ready — it is shown under your name in the upper-right corner of the console. This speeds up assistance.
