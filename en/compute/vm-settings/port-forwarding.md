# Port Forwarding

## Port Forwarding

Port forwarding redirects traffic from a port of a public IP address to a port of your VM. For example, you can forward external port 8080 to port 80 of the VM.

---

- On the **VM Settings** tab, select **Port Forwarding**. The list shows the public IP addresses of the VM and their networks.
- Click **Manage** next to the IP address. The public IP address page opens on the **Port Forwarding** tab, where you can add and remove rules.

![Port Forwarding Configuration](../../../images/vm-portforwading.png)

---

### Conclusion

Port forwarding lets you expose services running on your VM. Open only the ports that are really needed.
