# Change Hostname

## Change Hostname Settings

The hostname is the name of your virtual machine in the network. You can change it to match your naming conventions.

---

- On the **VM Settings** tab, select **Change Hostname**.
- Enter the new name in **Host Name/ Server Label** and click **Change Hostname**.
- This changes the hostname recognized when the server is installed. For active servers, you can also change the hostname by logging into the OS.

> [!WARNING]
> Changing the hostname will restart your virtual machine.

![Change Hostname Settings](../../../images/vm-settings-change-hostname.png)

---

### Conclusion

Use clear and consistent hostnames to identify servers more easily and integrate them with domain services.
