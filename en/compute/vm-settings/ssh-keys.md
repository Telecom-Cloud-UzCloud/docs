# SSH Keys

## SSH Key Access

SSH keys provide secure, passwordless access to your virtual machine.

---

- On the **VM Settings** tab, select **SSH Keys** to view the SSH keys of the VM.

> [!WARNING]
> You need to stop the instance to reset the SSH key.

![SSH Keys](../../../images/vm-ssh.png)

---

### Conclusion

Key-based authentication is more secure than passwords. Regularly review your SSH keys and remove the ones you no longer use.
